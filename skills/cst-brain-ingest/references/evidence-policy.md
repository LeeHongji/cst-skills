# Evidence policy

- Raw sources are immutable copies under `brain/raw/sources`.
- SHA256 is the source identity; filename is descriptive only.
- `candidate`: extracted or proposed, not yet trusted generally.
- `case-specific`: supported within one trace or case.
- `validated`: supported by two independent evidence references or explicit human approval.
- `contradicted` and `superseded` pages remain searchable for auditability.
- A source can support a claim, but a source page is not automatically a validated claim.
